Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Worth Asking.

Straightforward answers about what GENREV!™ is, who it serves, and how it works.

What is GENREV!™?

GENREV!™ is a commercial leadership resource for industrial B2B manufacturers and distributors under $50M in revenue. The work is built around three disciplines that drive profitable revenue growth: Forecasting, Strategy, and Performance Management. These are brought together through Sales Resource Management (SRM), a proactive operating discipline supported by a purpose-built platform and installed by the person who built it. Most organizations can find one of these. GENREV!™ brings all three.

Who is GENREV!™ designed for?

GENREV!™ is designed for B2B manufacturers and distributors that want to strengthen their sales effort, improve forecasting, drive margin performance, and build the organizational alignment that makes results repeatable. It is well suited for companies with an established customer base and a dedicated sales effort, whether a single contributor or a full team, that are looking for a practical, experienced approach to improving sales clarity, focus, and results. For very early-stage organizations with no sales history and purely opportunistic selling, simpler tools may be more appropriate during that initial phase.

What business challenges does GENREV!™ help address?

The challenges GENREV!™ addresses are common in manufacturing and distribution organizations of this size: sales forecasts that miss consistently, margins that erode without a clear explanation, a sales team that works hard but lacks structural focus, difficulty identifying where the best growth opportunities actually are, and leadership that lacks real visibility into what is driving or limiting performance. Sales is often the part of the business leaders feel least equipped to address. GENREV!™ provides the structure, accountability, and visibility to close that gap.

How is GENREV!™ different from hiring a full-time sales executive?

A full-time sales executive is a significant commitment in cost, in time, and in organizational change. GENREV!™ is available on a fractional, interim, or project basis, which means the organization gets experienced commercial leadership without the overhead of a permanent hire. It also brings a proven process and a purpose-built platform, installed by the person who built it, that stay with the organization after the engagement ends. The discipline that gets built is designed to be self-sustaining. The work is not dependent on one person remaining employed there.

How is working with GENREV!™ different from bringing in outside help?

GENREV!™ works alongside the leadership team and the sales team from day one, listening before acting, understanding the current state, and building from there. The goal is never to impose a generic framework or deliver a report and leave. The process and disciplines that are built during the engagement remain with the organization. What was built stays.

How does GENREV!™ support an organization's growth strategy?

By providing clear visibility into sales performance, future opportunities, and margin potential, GENREV!™ helps leadership teams align their sales effort with their actual growth objectives. Priorities, resources, and activities get focused where they will deliver the greatest business impact, not spread across whatever comes through the door.

How does GENREV!™ foster organizational alignment?

Sales performance rarely lives in one department. GENREV!™ provides a structured framework that helps sales, product management, engineering, operations, finance, and executive leadership operate from a shared understanding of priorities, progress, and financial impact. When everyone is working from the same picture, the conversation changes.

How does GENREV!™ help improve forecasting accuracy?

Forecasting improves when it is built on structure and accountability rather than optimism and habit. GENREV!™ brings a disciplined approach to understanding what is in the pipeline, what is real versus hopeful, and what the organization can actually count on. Customer-specific insights and profitability context give leadership a more honest picture of what to expect, and where to focus attention before the quarter closes.

How does GENREV!™ help manage sales opportunities?

GENREV!™ provides a clear, structured view of sales potential across key customers, industries, and account types. It helps leadership prioritize where the real growth opportunities are, both within the existing customer base and in targeted new business, and align that view with forecasting and margin objectives so effort goes where it matters most.

What business insights does GENREV!™ provide?

GENREV!™ provides visibility into historical sales, current performance, and future opportunities, organized by customer, industry, customer type, and account type. This allows leadership to identify high-value customers, assess profitability, and make informed decisions about where to focus the sales effort. The insights are designed to support leadership conversations, not replace them.

What is the Sales Health Index™ and why is it free?

The Sales Health Index™ is a private, structured self-assessment that gives sales leadership an honest picture of where their organization stands across the key dimensions of sales performance. It takes five minutes, requires no login, and no one but Chuck ever sees the responses. It is offered at no charge because the goal is genuine insight, not a sales pitch. If the results prompt a conversation, that is welcome. If they simply provide useful context, that is equally fine.

How does GENREV!™ compare to traditional CRM and BI tools?

GENREV!™ is Sales Resource Management (SRM), which is a different category of work than CRM or BI. CRM records activity. BI reports on what happened. SRM is proactive by design: it organizes forecasting, strategy, and performance management around a structured rhythm that turns data into decisions and decisions into action. The GENREV!™ platform includes the account-level notes, alerts, and dashboards organizations expect from those tools, but the real difference is the experienced leadership that knows how to use that information to move the business forward. GENREV!™ works whether a CRM is in place or not. The two are complementary, not competing.

How is GENREV!™ different from sales training programs?

Sales training teaches people how to sell: qualification, relationship building, objection handling, and similar skills delivered to individuals. GENREV!™ answers a different question: whether the trained behaviors are actually producing results. It is a management discipline, not a training program, organized around forecasting, strategy, and performance management at the organizational level. The two are complementary rather than competing. Many organizations invest in training and still cannot say with confidence whether their pipeline is real or their forecast is reliable. GENREV!™ closes that gap by managing performance after the training ends, so the investment in skill-building actually shows up in revenue and margin.

Can GENREV!™ be used with or without a CRM?

Yes. GENREV!™ is Sales Resource Management (SRM), not CRM, and does not require a CRM to be effective. Many organizations either do not have one, or find that their CRM does not provide the structured forecasting and leadership alignment they need. Even where a CRM is in place, leadership often encounters inconsistent use that makes it difficult to rely on that data alone. GENREV!™ addresses that gap directly and works alongside whatever systems are already in place.

Do I need a specific ERP system?

No. GENREV!™ is designed to work alongside existing systems, not replace them. It accepts historical sales data exported in common formats from virtually any ERP system. Once the initial setup is complete, organizations typically update sales data on a regular cadence to keep forecasting current. The integration is intentionally straightforward.

Where is GENREV!™ located, and do engagements require on-site work?

GENREV!™ is based in the New York Metro area and works with industrial B2B manufacturers and distributors nationally. Engagements are delivered through a mix of on-site and virtual work, with the balance adapted to what each situation requires. Running distributed and multi-location sales operations is well-established territory. It has been part of the work since the early 1990s.

What does an engagement typically look like?

Every engagement starts with a free conversation, no agenda, no pitch. If there appears to be a fit, the scope, structure, and timeline get defined together based on what the organization actually needs. From there, the work begins alongside the leadership and sales team, with clear accountability on both sides. Fractional and interim engagements bring all three dimensions of GENREV!™ together: experience, the SRM process, and the platform. Project-based engagements draw on that same experience, with GENREV!™ included when it fits the scope.

What kind of support is provided?

Support includes guided onboarding to ensure proper data alignment and platform configuration, collaborative assistance integrating the process into existing sales and performance management workflows, and ongoing access to experienced guidance to help leadership teams get the most from what has been built.

Can GENREV!™ be used outside of industrial B2B?

Yes. Although GENREV!™ was built around the principles and challenges of industrial B2B manufacturing and distribution, its structured approach to forecasting, margin management, and organizational alignment applies effectively in any B2B environment where sales planning, performance management, and cross-functional collaboration are priorities. Organizations in services, technology, and other complex sales environments have found the framework equally relevant.

What does it mean to generate revenue profitably?

The phrase reflects the core focus of everything GENREV!™ does: helping organizations drive sales growth while maintaining margin discipline and cross-functional alignment. Revenue without margin is activity, not progress. GENREV!™ helps leadership define clear growth objectives, align the sales effort accordingly, and execute with the structure, accountability, and visibility that makes growth both meaningful and repeatable.
