"We Need Sales Now."

February 10, 2026  |  Chuck Kitchen, Founder GENREV!™

It's a phrase that echoes through many organizations when the pressure is on. But here is a reality check: if it's impossible to instantly change a plant layout, it's impossible to "instant gratification" a sales turnaround.

Holding a sales team accountable without a strategy is like blaming a forklift driver for a bottleneck in a poorly designed warehouse. Speed does not fix a bad layout; it often leads to faster crashes.

Sales acceleration is about sales resource optimization. The gap between "sales now" and sustainable growth is bridged by alignment - identifying the friction in the process before fueling the fire.
