Year-End Results Stop Moving. Interpretation Shouldn't.

December 27, 2025  |  Chuck Kitchen, Founder GENREV!™

Late December is when most sales numbers stop moving.

What 𝘥𝘰𝘦𝘴 move right now is interpretation.

I’ve seen leaders spend this week either explaining results away, or quietly trying to understand what actually happened.

The difference matters.

Year-end is not just about whether targets were hit.

It’s about whether the outcomes were predictable.

Predictability tells you far more than raw performance:

  • Were the misses surprises or confirmations?
  • Did strong months mask weak fundamentals?
  • Did the forecast help, or just report?

From a leadership standpoint, this week is one of the few moments where hindsight is still fresh and defensiveness is lower.

That makes it a powerful time to ask better questions-before the calendar resets and optimism takes over.
