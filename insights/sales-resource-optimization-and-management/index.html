SALES RESOURCE OPTIMIZATION AND MANAGEMENT

March 2024  |  Chuck Kitchen, Founder GENREV!™

“We need sales now”. This phrase plays out in everyday business. What does it mean? Salespeople, feeling pressure, go about making calls (assumes a call = order) and management fuels the fire by wielding their authority. Yet, what’s the plan? Let’s consider a slightly different situation.

I wonder what management would say if someone said, “let’s change the layout of the plant or warehouse (or any area of a business) now”? Of course, immediate reactions range from asking why, to noting how involved such a project would be (time, money, resources and risks). Back to “We need sales now”.

It is interesting how managers/leaders wield their authority knowingly or not, demanding instant sales with “facts” such as (but not limited to):

  • We know the customers.

  • All we need to do is ask for more.

  • Our customers buy from our competitors, so let’s change that.

  • Get out in front of them and they will buy.

  • It’s been 90 days and it’s time to make rain (gotta love that one).​

STOP the madness! If you can’t instantly change a plant layout, what makes you think sales is instant gratification? NEWS FLASH: IT’S NOT - yes, we said it’s not.

All too often, business leaders make statements such as, “They know what they need to do”, to “Don’t complicate it”. Really? So, do you need sales, a new plant layout or…. a strategy - based on effectively utilizing valuable, limited resources (sales resource optimization) and managing the process? hopefully the latter. PS: This scenario plays out in small and large, as well as public and private business. we know, because we've been a part of it. Interesting.
