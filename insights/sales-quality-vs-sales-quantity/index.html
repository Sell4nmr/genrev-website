Sales Quality vs. Sales Quantity

January 2026  |  Chuck Kitchen, Founder GENREV!™

Jan 2026: 𝐒𝐚𝐥𝐞𝐬 𝐐𝐮𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐭𝐲 𝐯𝐬. 𝐒𝐚𝐥𝐞𝐬 𝐐𝐮𝐚𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐭𝐲

Sales conversations commonly default to activity, such as:

  • Calls made.
  • Meetings scheduled.
  • Opportunities entered.

What often gets less attention is quality. Are opportunities well understood? Is customer intent clear? Do numbers reflect real movement, or just motion?

Whether you're an individual contributor or leading a team, it’s worth considering a simple discipline: before adding activity, make sure what’s already in motion is worth pursuing.

Anyone willing to share how they assess quality early, before activity starts to compound?
